A well-presented two-bedroom ground floor apartment, improved throughout by the current owner and ready to move into. With no restriction on letting, the property will appeal equally to first-time buyers, downsizers and investors.

The accommodation comprises an entrance hall with storage and a bright living room. The fitted kitchen has space for appliances and offers scope for a purchaser to update to their own taste. There are two bedrooms — a generous double and a second bedroom ideal as a guest room or home office — along with a refitted bathroom with shower over the bath.

Externally, the apartment enjoys beautiful, well-maintained communal gardens and the benefit of a garage.

The location is a particular strength: a doctors’ surgery and pharmacy sit directly opposite, with a regular bus route immediately outside. Musgrove Park Hospital, Taunton town centre, the railway station and the M5 are all easily reached.

Being on the ground floor, the apartment offers step-free access throughout.
